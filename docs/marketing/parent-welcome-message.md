## 繁體中文

**主旨：Camp WOW 開放報名（2027 年 1 月 28 日至 31 日）**

各位家長您好：

感謝您對 Camp WOW（World of Outdoor Wonders）的關注。我們的第一梯營隊現在開放報名。

**營隊資訊**
- 2027 年 1 月 28 日至 31 日（週四至週日），招收 10–14 歲營員，地點在苗栗三灣的斑比跳跳。營隊期間由我們包場。
- 每位營員 NT$35,000，已含臺中來回遊覽車專車。首梯為試辦營，限額 30 名營員。
- 營員住在露營車，三人一車，每日三餐。駐點護理師日夜在營，隊輔全程陪伴營員。

**集合與返程**
- 週四 08:00 在台中林酒店集合，遊覽車專車 08:30 出發。週日 10:00 自營地出發，預計約 11:30 返抵集合地點。

**報名方式**
1. 請至 〔官網網址〕 填寫報名表。
2. 請與孩子一起閱讀服務契約與營隊須知，並在最後一頁簽名。
3. 請填寫營員健康調查表，供護理師使用。
4. 〔訂金金額與尾款繳費日期〕

**請您留意**
- 報到時發燒的營員不上車。患有嚴重氣喘或其他重大疾病的孩子無法參加本營隊；若不確定，請在報名前先與我們聯繫。
- 營隊期間手機等裝置由隊輔妥善保管。您隨時可以聯繫我們，也能在專屬相簿中看到孩子的照片。
- 營隊若因任何原因取消，或因颱風、地震等天災而無法舉辦，已繳費用全額退還。若是家長需要解約，則依官網公布的退費表辦理。
- 行李清單請見官網。主要物品：保暖冬季外套、刷毛衣等保暖中層衣物、毛巾與浴巾、盥洗用品、水壺。

有任何問題，請透過 〔LINE ID〕 或直接回覆此訊息與我們聯繫。本營隊與持有執照的旅行社 〔承辦旅行社名稱〕 合作辦理。

期待與孩子見面！

Camp WOW 營隊團隊
創世紀青少年戶外探索 × 斑比跳跳

## English

**Subject: Camp WOW, 28–31 January 2027: registration is open**

Dear parents,

Thank you for your interest in Camp WOW (World of Outdoor Wonders). Registration for our first camp is now open.

**The camp**
- 28–31 January 2027 (Thursday to Sunday), for campers aged 10 to 14, at Bambi Glamping in Sanwan, Miaoli. The camp has the whole venue to itself.
- NT$35,000 per camper, with the charter bus from Taichung included. This first camp is a pilot with 30 places.
- Campers stay in camper vans, three to a van, with three meals a day. A registered nurse is on site day and night, and counselors are with the campers throughout.

**Getting there**
- Meet at The Lin Hotel, Taichung at 08:00 on Thursday. The bus leaves at 08:30. On Sunday the bus leaves camp at 10:00 and is back at about 11:30.

**To register**
1. Fill in the registration form at [website address].
2. Read the service contract and the Camp Notice and Agreement with your child, and sign the last page.
3. Complete the Camper Health Form for our nurse.
4. [Deposit amount and the date the balance is due.]

**What to know**
- Campers with a fever at check-in do not board the bus. Campers with severe asthma or another serious medical condition cannot join this camp. If you are unsure, ask us before you register.
- Phones and other devices are kept safely by counselors during camp. You can reach us at any time, and you will see photos of your child in a private album.
- If the camp is cancelled for any reason, or a typhoon, earthquake or other natural disaster stops it, you receive a full refund. If your family needs to cancel, the refund follows the table on our website.
- The packing list is on the website. The essentials: warm winter coat, fleece layers, towel, toiletries, water bottle.

Questions? Message us on [LINE ID] or reply to this message. The camp is organized with [travel agency name], our licensed travel agency.

We look forward to meeting your child.

The Camp WOW team
Genesis Youth Adventures × Bambi Glamping
