What we need from Bambi’s team during camp

Camp WOW · Thursday 28 to Sunday 31 January 2027 · 30 campers · we bring the counselors, the nurse and the camp director

In short. Four named people carry the camp: a camp contact by day, a night duty person for each of the three nights, the kayak coach and the tram driver. Around them we need a few people at set times: reception and housekeeping on arrival and departure, someone at the campfire and the cinema, and a maintenance person on call. At the busiest moment, Thursday afternoon, that is about four Bambi people at once, not counting the kitchen. The kitchen is Bambi’s own call.

This is my estimate from our timetable. Bambi knows its own staff and may combine roles, for example the contact and the reception, or the night duty person and the gate. What matters is that each job has a name next to it.

4. Not on this list

The kitchen: breakfast at 08:00, lunch at 12:00 on Thursday, 12:30 on Friday and 12:45 on Saturday, dinner at 18:00 every evening, warm drinks on Thursday at 16:00, a snack on Friday at 20:10 and the Wagyu dinner on Saturday. How many cooks that takes is Bambi’s decision. Nobody is needed at the pool, which is closed for the four days, but someone should check on arrival day that it is locked.

5. Questions for the meeting

  1. Are these people part of the price we agreed with Bambi, or charged on top?
  2. Who is the named camp contact, and who is the deputy? We need both mobile numbers for the emergency card.
  3. Who is the night duty person each night, and where do they sleep?
  4. Is one kayak coach enough for ten on the water, or does Bambi need a second person or a safety boat? Who is the backup coach, and what are their qualifications?
  5. Who drives the tram, and is that person also needed elsewhere at 16:40 on Thursday?
  6. Who sets up and runs the screen and sound, and who provides the licensed film?
  7. Can a maintenance person be on call from 07:00 to 22:00?
  8. Which Bambi staff are trained in CPR, and where is the AED?

Before camp: a walk round the site with the camp contact, so everyone knows where the assembly point, the AED and the shut-offs are. Bambi also gives us copies of the kayak coach’s certificates, the tram’s inspection record and the insurance for the lake, the kayaks and the tram.
