Camp WOW: questions for Dave and James

Winter camp at Bambi Glamping · Thursday 28 to Sunday 31 January 2027 · from Grant

The outline these questions belong to. 30 campers aged 10 to 14, with their counselors, a camp lead and a nurse. The bus leaves The Lin Hotel, Taichung, at 08:30 on Thursday and arrives at about 10:00. Kayaking and the tram are on Thursday. We have the whole site to ourselves for the four days, with our base in zones A and B beside the big lawn. Wake-up is 07:00, breakfast 08:00 and lights out 22:00. On Sunday the bus leaves camp at 10:00. The full day-by-day plan is in the planning document.

1Staff hours. Can your team cover breakfast at 08:00, lunch at 12:00 on Thursday, 12:30 on Friday and 12:45 on Saturday, dinner at 18:00 each evening, the kayak coach on Thursday 13:30 to 15:45, the tram on Thursday 16:40 to 17:40, and the campfires on Thursday and Saturday evening?

2Vans. Can we have the east end of B and the north end of A, about 15 vans in one group, for all three nights?

3The hall. On a wet day all our activities move into the main dining hall. Can the tables be moved to make space?

4The covered activity area. Is there an outdoor covered activity area? If so, how big is it?

5The lawn. May we play soft-ball games and frisbee on it, and hammer about 60 tent pegs in for the shelters? Your guest notice does not allow hard balls or throwing games.

6The lake and the tram. How many boats and coaches, and how many campers at once? Is there a safety boat? How many seats on the tram and how long is one run? Is a second lake session possible on Saturday 11:10 to 12:40?

7Sunday. We give the keys back at 08:30, the campers gather for the prize-giving, and then we head back. With no other guests that weekend, is 08:30 still when you need the keys?

Safety and emergencies have their own short list (the Camp WOW safety plan), for a separate conversation.
