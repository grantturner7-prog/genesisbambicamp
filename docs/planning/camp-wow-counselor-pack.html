Teams, squads and who does what

Thirty campers make three teams of ten: Yellow, Orange and Green, the colours of the Decathlon bibs. Each team chooses its own name and mascot on Thursday afternoon.

Each team is two squads of five: Y1 and Y2, O1 and O2, G1 and G2. Team games are played ten against ten. Squad games give six squads, which means three matches at once or six stations with nobody waiting.

The Director sets the squads before camp, balanced for age, and every camper has a buddy inside their squad.

The ten adults

Camp Director
Runs the day. Keeps the one clock and the whistle at every timed activity, hosts the evenings, makes the weather call. Base: HQ Palms.
Nurse
Health, medicine and first aid, day and night. At the Dock Gate whenever boats are on the water, otherwise at HQ Palms or with the largest group.
Six squad counselors
One for each squad, called by the squad’s name: “Y1’s counselor”. With their five campers from the wake-up knock to lights out. They count, coach, time and score their own squad.
Floater 1
Kit and set-up. Lays out each activity before it starts, referees, tends the fire, keeps the shore watch on the Dock.
Floater 2
Scores and set-up. Works with Floater 1 on every set-up, hosts craft stations, collects every score slip and keeps the Color War board.
Bambi’s team
The kayak coach, the tram driver, the kitchen, the Star Cinema projector, and the front desk.

Eight radios: the Director, the nurse, Floater 1, Floater 2, and the counselors of Y1, O1 and G1, with one spare that goes to the Dock on lake days. Every adult also carries a phone, a whistle, the squad first-aid kit and a lanyard with the day’s timetable.

Camp basics

Count before you move
Each squad counselor counts five before every move and after it. When the Director calls “Squads?”, six counselors answer “Five!”. A count by name from the roster is done three times a day: at the morning van check, at lights out, and whenever we board the bus.
Two whistle signals
One long whistle: stop and look at the Director. Three short whistles: walk to HQ Palms.
Water
No camper is at a lake edge, on the Dock or past the flag line by the pool without a counselor. On the water, life jackets stay clipped and Bambi’s coach is in charge.
Fire
Counselors light, feed and put out the fire. Campers sit on the logs.
After dark
Campers carry a torch. Counselors wear a headlamp and a bright vest. Groups walk together with a counselor at the back.
Adults and campers
No counselor is alone with one camper out of sight of others. Van checks are done by two counselors.
Hurt or unwell
Small things are treated on the spot from the squad kit. For anything else the counselor radios the nurse. The nurse and the Director decide together whether to phone the family.
A camper is not at a count
It is nearly always the toilet, the van or another squad. The squad counselor says the name on the radio. Every counselor checks their own group, one floater checks the vans and toilets, and the other walks the lake side and the pool side. The programme carries on. If the camper has not turned up once the camp has been checked, the Director takes over and decides what happens next with Bambi’s front desk.
Thunder
Everyone walks to the Hall or the vans and waits there until the Director gives the all-clear.
A real emergency
Phone 119, then tell the Director. Bambi’s front desk opens the gate. The camp’s address and the phone numbers are on the back of every lanyard.

The board

  • The Color War board is three columns on poster paper on the Hall wall, beside the door. Floater 2 adds every result at the next meal.
  • Counselors hand score slips to Floater 2 straight after each activity. A slip names the activity, the squads or teams, and the result. Nothing is added up in anyone’s head.
  • Tied squads or teams both take the points of the higher place.
  • From Saturday dinner the board is covered. The final totals are shown for the first time at the prize-giving.

Minute by minute

0–3 minShow a finished bracelet, then the two moves, slowly, on a clipboard held up.
3–8 minThe core. Fold the short piece in half. Tape the fold to the top of a clipboard, leaving a loop a thumb wide above the tape. Wrap the two tails round the wrist to measure, add two fingers, and tie both tails in one knot there. That knot is the button.
8–24 minThe weave. Lay the middle of the long piece under the core, just below the tape. Left end over the core, right end over the left end, behind the core and up through the left loop. Pull tight. Then the same from the right. Left, right, left, right, down to the button knot.

  • The weave twists into a spiral: the camper started from the same side twice. It still makes a bracelet. Let it be.
  • The weave is loose: pull each knot up against the one before.
  • Too short for the wrist: the button knot was tied without the two extra fingers. Untie it and move it down.

What this plan fixes, so that you can change it

The working plan left these open. They are now decided, and each one can be changed without touching the rest.

Team colours
Yellow, Orange and Green. They follow whichever three bib colours Decathlon has on the shelf.
Staff
Ten adults: the Director, the nurse, six squad counselors and two floaters with fixed jobs (kit, and scores).
The scoring scale
Squad games 6 to 1, team games 10, 7, 4, the finale at double points, a spirit award of 5. The van check and the sports league score as in the working plan.
The advance car
The two floaters drive the kit up early on Thursday and are on site by 08:30. The deck of van B05 is the kit store.
Every position on the maps
Drawn on our 3D model of Bambi’s map. Walk the stations on Thursday morning and move any that do not suit the ground.
Turn orders
Thursday kayak order Yellow, Green, Orange; tram order Yellow, Orange, Green; Saturday kayak order Green, Orange, Yellow.
The Orienteering word
ADVENTURES, with the ten control places listed on the sheet. The bonus bearing is measured on the morning.
The Amazing Race tasks
The seven tasks, their places, and the gold times.
Sports fixtures
Each squad plays each sport twice over the two days and never plays its own team’s other squad.
The skit theme
How our mascot saved Camp WOW, with three props a team.
The Glow Night Quest
Six stations, a six-word sentence, and the cinema tickets in the treasure box.
The Egg Drop
A 2 m drop onto the main path, and the kit in each bag.
Phones and medicine
Phones come back at boarding on Sunday. Medicine goes back to families at the hotel.
The health form
It needs one more line, “can swim 25 m: yes or no”, so that the coach knows who paddles beside him.

Print six at A3 and laminate them. The numbers are the ten orienteering controls. The same map serves the Amazing Race.
