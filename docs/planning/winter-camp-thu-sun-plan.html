Winter camp, Thursday to Sunday

A plan for sharing the site with Bambi’s other guests · Genesis × Bambi · draft for discussion with Dave · 22 November 2026

The short answer

This works. Bambi’s own guest timetable leaves us real gaps, and with 30 campers we only need a corner of the site. The work is in fixing where we are at which hour, and in having a second indoor space for bad weather. My recommendations:

  1. Use Thursday for everything that needs the whole site: the lake, the tram, the campfire and the cinema. The site is ours that day.
  2. From Friday afternoon, keep the camp in one corner: the north-east vans, the central lawn and one covered indoor space. Counselors’ vans go on the west and south sides as a buffer. The guests go to the west side of the camp.
  3. Treat five places as shared (reception, dining hall, kayak dock and tram, the campfire and cinema lawn, the public restrooms) and set our times there around the guests’ times.
  4. Use the guests’ gap. Overnight guests leave by 10:30 and the next ones arrive at 14:00, and they get no lunch. So 10:30 to 14:00 is when the site is nearly empty: our lake, tram and lawn window on Friday, Saturday and Sunday.
  5. Eat off Bambi’s schedule: breakfast at 07:15, lunch about 12:00, dinner at 17:00 to 17:15, ahead of their 18:00 to 20:00 dinner.
  6. The weather risk is the evening. When it rains, Bambi moves its guests’ cinema and snack into the dining hall. We need the covered activity area (or the back of the hall) agreed in writing, and a rule for who gets the hall on a wet night.
  7. Sunday needs a decision. The ride out took 1.5 hours (08:30 to 10:00). To be back at The Lin Hotel by 10:30, the bus has to leave camp at about 09:00, not 09:30.

What I assumed (please correct me)

  • Dates: Thursday 28 to Sunday 31 January 2027 (one day later than the Wednesday 27 to Saturday 30 we had). Four days, three nights. 30 campers, 10 to 14 years old, 1 counselor to 4 campers, so 8 counselors, plus the nurse and a camp lead.
  • Exclusive use: all of Thursday. From Friday the site is shared. Guests arrive from 14:00 on Friday and Saturday, and leave by 10:30 on Saturday and Sunday. I don’t yet know whether Friday morning is also ours alone.

  • The pool is closed to the camp in winter and I have not planned anything there.
  • Van numbers, distances and the size of the lawn come from our traced copy of Bambi’s official map. Walk them on site before we promise anything.

Why this arrangement

  • The campers are farthest from the entrance road. Counselor vans sit between them and the guests’ evening lawn (west) and the entrance road (south), so nobody reaches a camper van without passing a counselor.
  • They are close to what we use most: the kayak dock is 33 to 60 m from A10 to A14, the campfire and cinema about 60 to 90 m, and the open lawn is in the middle of the cluster.
  • The vans have private bathrooms, so campers do not need the public restrooms at night.
  • Bambi’s own rule helps: its FAQ says only that day’s overnight guests are admitted and visitors are turned away, and that the gate is closed at 23:00 except in an emergency. Everyone else on site is a registered guest or staff. Ask Dave to confirm this is still current.

The core zone: simple rules

  • Mark it: cones or tape across the two ways in, a banner at each, and a map board at the lawn.
  • No camper leaves the core zone without a counselor. Buddy pairs, always, including to the restrooms.
  • Campers wear the camp T-shirt or a colour band, so any adult on site can see at a glance who is ours.
  • Headcount at fixed moments: after every move between places, at meals, before and after every rotation, at lights-out and again at 23:00. Two counselors count independently and the numbers must match.
  • Two counselors on night duty, one awake in the cluster, the other walking the boundary. The nurse sleeps in the camper cluster.
  • Ask Dave’s staff to tell us before entering the core zone and to use the same two ways in, and to tell guests at check-in that a youth camp is on site.

2. Weather and the indoor space

What we know and what we need to ask

Our own site already promises a rain plan in “the Bambi Grand Dining Hall and the covered activity area”. The hall is a glass-walled room with big windows onto the palms. I do not know its size, whether it is split into two sides, whether the tables move, or where the covered area is. Those are the questions for Dave (section 6). Without them, plan like this:

  1. First choice, the covered activity area: big-group sessions, builds and experiments (it needs power, light and side walls against wind).
  2. Second choice, the back side of the dining hall in its free windows: about 09:00 to 14:30 and 16:00 to 17:00, never during Bambi’s meals. A good size for 30 would be a clear floor of about 8 by 10 m with tables pushed to the walls.
  3. Third choice, small groups with a counselor in the vans, quiet games only. Food stays out of the vans (the van rules).
  4. On Friday and Saturday nights, assume the hall is not ours after 19:30 if it rains. Our evening block must work in the covered area, or in the hall before 19:30.

6. Questions for Dave

The first eight decide whether the plan works. The rest can follow.

Needed first

  1. Which hours is the site ours alone? All of Thursday? Friday morning until the 14:00 arrivals? Are there guests on Wednesday night, and will the vans be ready by 10:30 on Thursday?
  2. Which vans can we hold? B06 to B12 and A10 to A14 for campers, B01 to B05 and A05, A06, A08 for counselors, with the guests in D to G (and the lower A vans left empty if possible)?
  3. How many guests on Friday and Saturday nights? Any large groups, parties or events, and is alcohol allowed near our corner?
  4. The dining hall. Is it split into two sides, and can we use the back one? Can we move tables? How many people does it hold, is it heated, and are there power sockets and a screen? Which hours can we count on?
  5. The wet-weather evening. Where do we go when Bambi moves its cinema and snack into the hall? Where is the covered activity area, and how big, how enclosed, how warm?
  6. Can we eat off the guests’ times? Breakfast 07:15, lunch about 12:00, dinner 17:00 to 17:15 (Saturday 17:15 to 18:15).
  7. Kayaks and tram. How many boats and coaches, and how many campers at once? Tram seats and guide? Can we have both on Thursday afternoon and on Saturday and Sunday mornings?
  8. Sunday timing. The bus leaves camp at about 09:00. Is that all right with keys, breakfast and the next guests?

Then

  1. Is Bambi’s weekend timetable (14:00 check-in, 15:00 tea, 18:00 to 20:00 dinner, 19:30 cinema, 23:00 gate) the winter one?
  2. Thursday and Friday meals: which dinner, which lunch dish and which breakfast on which day? Allergen details per dish, and for the standard Saturday and Sunday buffet?
  3. Is the pool closed and out of reach (covered, drained or fenced) for everyone in winter?
  4. Night security: who is on duty, are there cameras, who holds the master keys, and who answers the phone after 23:00?
  5. Will Bambi’s staff tell guests at check-in that a youth camp is on site, and agree not to enter our corner without telling us first?
  6. Is the Airstream snack shop open on our days, and at what hours?
  7. Campfire: who supplies the wood and the fire-keeper, and can we run the Thursday cinema on Bambi’s screen and projector?
  8. Does the Friday-night business day change the price?
