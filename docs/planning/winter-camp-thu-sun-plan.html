Your question: is 21:15 to 06:30 enough sleep?

Yes, with care. It gives about 9¼ hours in bed. Sleep-medicine guidance is 9 to 12 hours for ages 6 to 12 and 8 to 10 hours for teenagers, so this is comfortable for the 13 and 14 year olds and at the low end for the 10 to 12 year olds. For three nights that is fine if we protect it:

  • A quiet half hour before lights out: showers, the van check, no games.
  • The rest in the vans after lunch stays in every day.
  • The Friday evening snack is light and finishes by 20:50.
  • Bambi’s own film runs to about 21:20 and its snack to 22:00, about 100 m from our vans, so windows stay closed on Friday and Saturday.
  • If the campers look tired on Saturday, move wake-up to 07:00 and breakfast to 07:30. Nothing else needs to change.

Your earlier worry was right about my first draft: lights out at 22:00 with a 07:00 start was too little for the younger ones.

Safety basics that stay

  • Headcount by name from a printed roster after every move, at meals and at lights out.
  • If a camper is missing: everyone stops, a count by name, then two counselors check the water first (the dock, the lake edge, the pool), then the van, then the last place seen. The lead tells Bambi’s front desk.
  • Water: nobody goes to the lake edge or the pool without a counselor. The pool is out of bounds for the camp. Bambi lists it as open all year, so guests may be using it.
  • Kayaking in January: no kayaking in rain, drizzle, thunder or strong wind (Bambi’s rule). Agree a cold limit with the coach and the nurse, and change into dry clothes straight after.
  • After dark: every camper has a torch (packing list), counselors wear a headlamp and a bright vest, and groups walk with a counselor at the front and the back.
  • Campfire: counselors tend the fire, everyone sits on the log ring, water and a burn kit are beside it.
  • Adults: no counselor is alone with one camper out of sight. Van checks are done by two counselors.
  • Emergencies: call 119, then the lead, then the family. Ask Dave who opens the gate after 23:00 and where the AED is.
