# Photo audit

Rule: every photo on the site must show exactly the place or activity it is attached to.
Where Bambi's official site has no such photo, the slot is left empty.

Method: all 605 photos on Bambi's official site (76 pages) were indexed. For each of eight
topics a reviewer looked at the candidates and at the photos already in use, and a second
reviewer independently re-opened every proposed photo and checked it against its caption,
the venue, and the content rules (no alcohol or bar signage, no text-overlay graphics,
nothing unsuitable for a youth camp, unit numbers read for every zone photo).

## What changed

| Place / activity | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eco-Safari Tram | Two camper-van photos, no tram | The tram on its route (the only clean tram photo the venue has) |
| Dining hall | Hall and pizza only; one frame with drink promotion cards | Buffet eggs, fruit and yogurt, two breakfast plates, pizza, plus the hall; food strip on the panel and with the menu |
| Wild Waterbird Lake | Camper vans, no birds | Egret roost, egrets in the treetops and in flight, cattle egrets, ducks and geese |
| Entrance & check-in | Meadow and lakeside vans captioned as arrival | Honest views inside the grounds (no gate photo exists) |
| Restrooms, AED & First Aid | Photo of the dining hall | No photo (none exists); the Photos tab is hidden |
| Aurora Pool & Airstream | Included frames with the bar open, bar neon, a child jumping in, a child running on the deck | Sunset overview, the Airstream with its hatch closed, the curved steps, children in the shallow end |
| Kayak lake | Included an adult glamour-style shot | Boarding deck with kayaks, paddlers in life jackets, the lake |
| Star Cinema | A promo graphic with text over it | A real screening at night, the screen at dusk, the lawn by day |
| Campfire | A framed graphic showing fried snacks | The brick fire pit alight (film credits cropped out), marshmallows, the pit by day |
| Camper vans & zones | One Zone F photo filed under Zone E; one uncertain "Zone F" photo; children's faces | Every zone photo has its unit number confirmed; face-free interiors |
| Winter hero image | Summer-green lakeside photo | Bald cypress in copper colour by the lakeside vans |
| Craft Studio card | No photo | Hands shaping a moss ball |

## Photos the venue does not have (to be taken on site or requested from Bambi)

- Tram: boarding point, children riding, the tram at dusk, any landscape-format or large photo.
- Dining: a wide shot of the open buffet, the evening buffet, a non-pizza chef-led lunch, campers cooking, special-diet trays, the signature family dinner as it is served to campers (the site uses Bambi's own Wagyu platter photo, `wagyu-platter`, chosen because it shows no alcohol; most of Bambi's dinner photos include wine or beer and were rejected), s'mores.
- Entrance gate and sign, the check-in unit, restrooms / showers / changing rooms, the water dispenser, the AED, parking, the charter bus.
- Pool: a bright-daylight overview showing the depth zones, pool relays, the lifeguard, the Airstream working as a snack shop, the snack coupons.
- Kayak: the coach briefing, life jackets being fitted, camp-age children paddling.
- Evening: a group around the fire, skits, trivia, the night walk.
- Camp programme with no venue photo at all: shelter building, paracord, orienteering, capture the flag, the camp T-shirt and album. These cards stay photo-free.

## To confirm with Bambi

  galleries (the tram photo and the double kayak) and one has no watermark (Zone F deck at dusk).
- A few approved photos show guests, including children, at small size or from behind.
